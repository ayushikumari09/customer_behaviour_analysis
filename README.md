# customer_behaviour_analysis
Customer Behavior Analysis using Python, SQL, and Power BI to uncover purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores customer shopping behavior to identify patterns in purchasing decisions, product preferences, spending habits, and customer satisfaction. It combines Python-based data analysis, SQL queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard to turn raw data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Understand customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
- Identify popular product categories and customer preferences.
- Analyze purchase frequency, discounts, and promotional influences.
- Explore differences in customer demographics and shopping habits.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction and subscription behavior.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
